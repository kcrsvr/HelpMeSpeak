Privacy Policy for HelpMeSpeak

Last updated: September 12, 2026

HelpMeSpeak (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is a communication and learning aid designed to help non-verbal and minimally verbal children — including autistic children — practice and use words. This policy explains how the app handles information.

We built HelpMeSpeak with children’s privacy as a first principle. The short version: the app stores everything on your own device and does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. HelpMeSpeak has no user accounts, no login, and no server. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics or tracking software.

Information Stored On Your Device

To function, the app saves the following only on the device where it is installed, and never sends it anywhere:

  • Child profile details you create (such as a name or nickname and a chosen avatar or theme).
  • Words, categories, pictures, and audio recordings that a caregiver adds.
  • App settings and a caregiver PIN, and how often words are used (to power features such as “Recently Used”).

This information never leaves the device. We cannot see it, and it is not backed up to any server operated by us.

Device Permissions

The app may ask for these permissions, used only for the stated purpose and only when a caregiver chooses to use the related feature:

  • Microphone — so a caregiver can record how a word sounds.
  • Photos / Camera — so a caregiver can add a familiar picture for a word.

Recordings and photos are saved on the device and are not transmitted anywhere. Denying these permissions simply disables the related optional feature.

Children’s Privacy

HelpMeSpeak is intended to be set up and managed by a parent, caregiver, or educator. Because the app collects no personal information and transmits no data off the device, it is designed to comply with children’s privacy laws such as the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including protections for children.

Deleting Your Data

Because all data lives on the device, you are always in control:

  • Delete an individual profile, word, category, or recording from within the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

Changes to This Policy

If the app changes in a way that affects how information is handled — for example, if an optional cloud backup or sync feature is added in a future version — we will update this policy and describe the change here before that feature is enabled.
